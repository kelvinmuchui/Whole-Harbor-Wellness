- Unmixed lyophilized vials should be kept cool, dry, and refrigerated in accordance with product labeling.

- Whole Harbor Wellness is not responsible for potency loss, degradation, or spoilage resulting from recipient failure to promptly retrieve delivered parcels or store products under recommended conditions.

## 6. Cold-Chain Shipping, Delivery & Return Policy

### 6.1 Dispatch Schedule
To preserve thermal integrity and prevent temperature-sensitive parcels from remaining in unconditioned weekend sorting hubs, cold-chain orders typically dispatch **Monday through Thursday** via insulated bio-thermo packaging equipped with phase-change cooling packs. Priority 24/7 transit monitoring is maintained for active shipments.

### 6.2 Cold-Chain Delivery Guarantee & Refunds
Due to the temperature-sensitive, sterile, and perishable nature of peptide formulations, **all shipped orders are final sale and non-returnable** once dispatched, except under our Cold-Chain Delivery Guarantee:
- Qualifying cold-chain shipments are covered against **verified carrier loss**, **transit damage**, or **confirmed thermal transit failure** when shipped to an accurate, deliverable recipient address provided at checkout.
- To file a claim, notify **support@wholeharborwellness.com** within **24 hours of delivery** (or expected delivery date for lost parcels) with your Order Number (`WH-ORD-...`) and photographic documentation of packaging/vial condition.
